# FOIA Request Index

## Summary of Requests

All requests are made pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-101 et seq.

### FOIA-01: Financial Baseline
**Records Period:** January 1, 2023 through present  
**Purpose:** Establish the financial baseline and identify all funds, accounts, recurring vendors, revenue sources, and major expenditure categories  
**Location:** `01_FOIA-01-Financial-Baseline/`

**Records Requested:**
- Annual budgets and amended budgets
- General ledgers and expenditure/revenue ledgers
- Check registers and warrant registers
- Monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements
- Budget-versus-actual reports and year-end financial reports
- Financial reports presented to the Library Board
- Records identifying bank, investment, and other financial accounts

---

### FOIA-02: Cards, Purchases, and Reimbursements
**Records Period:** January 1, 2023 through present  
**Purpose:** Allow each card or purchasing transaction to be matched against supporting documentation and approvals  
**Location:** `02_FOIA-02-Cards-Purchases-Reimbursements/`

**Records Requested:**
- Credit card and debit card statements
- Records identifying cardholders and authorized users
- Itemized receipts and invoices
- Purchase orders and payment vouchers
- Approval documentation and reimbursement records
- Mileage, travel, meal, lodging, conference, and training expenses
- Online retail purchases (Amazon, Walmart, Sam's Club, Office Depot, etc.)
- Refunds, credits, chargebacks, and returned merchandise records

---

## Standard FOIA Request Language

All requests include the following standard provisions:

- Request electronic production where readily available
- Request native spreadsheets or accounting files where practical
- Ask that exempt portions be redacted rather than withholding entire records
- Request identification of specific statutory exemptions for each withholding or redaction
- Ask for written confirmation when no responsive records exist
- Ask the custodian to identify another public custodian if records are held elsewhere
- Request itemized estimate before reproduction costs exceed $25
