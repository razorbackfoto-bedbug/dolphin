# FOIA-05: Policies, Controls, and Board Oversight Analysis Framework

## Purpose

Compare written controls and board oversight requirements against actual transactions revealed in FOIA-01 through FOIA-04. Identify gaps between policy and practice.

## Analysis Strategy

### Step 1: Understand the Control Environment

From FOIA-05 documents, map out:

1. **Who has authority?**
   - Approval limits by person and amount
   - Signature authority
   - Card issuance authority
   - Fund transfer authority

2. **What are the rules?**
   - Purchasing policy
   - Approval requirements
   - Documentation requirements
   - Conflict of interest rules
   - Donation handling

3. **What oversight exists?**
   - Board review frequency
   - Financial reporting schedule
   - Audit requirements
   - Internal controls

### Step 2: Compare Policy to Practice

Using transactions identified in FOIA-01 through FOIA-04:

- **Large purchases:** Were they approved per policy? Was board approval obtained if required?
- **Card charges:** Who was the cardholder? Were they authorized? Are charges within policy?
- **Reimbursements:** Do they follow the policy? Are receipts documented per policy?
- **Donations:** Are they handled per policy? Are restrictions honored per policy?
- **Transfers:** Are they authorized per policy?
- **Fund expenditures:** Are Foundation Fund expenditures within policy?

### Step 3: Identify Gaps

When actual transactions deviate from policy:

- Is the deviation a one-time exception or a pattern?
- Is there documented approval for the exception?
- Is the exception reasonable or concerning?

## Key Documents to Organize

### Policies
- Purchasing policy (current and all prior versions)
- Credit card policy

- Cash handling policy
- Reimbursement policy
- Travel policy
- Procurement/bidding policy
- Conflicts of interest policy
- Donation policy
- Foundation Fund policy
- Asset control policy
- Internal control policy

### Approval Authority
- Chart showing who can approve what amount
- Current signature cards for bank accounts
- Current cardholder list and authorization
- Board-delegated authority (if any)

### Board Oversight
- Meeting minutes (all Board meetings)
- Board resolutions
- Financial reports to Board
- Board approval of expenditures or policies
- Claims lists or payment approvals

### Audit and Control
- Audit reports
- Management letters
- Corrective actions taken
- Internal control procedures

## Red Flags in Policies and Controls

Watch for (document but do not assume misconduct):

**In Policies:**
- Missing or vague policies
- Policies not updated in years
- Policies that are less stringent than standard practice
- Policies with excessive approval limits
- Policies with inadequate documentation requirements
- No audit or internal control requirements

**In Practice (compared to policy):**
- Purchases approved by someone without authority
- Purchases without required documentation
- Spending above stated limits without exception
- Reimbursements without receipts
- Donations accepted with restrictions but expenditures ignoring restrictions
- Card charges by unauthorized cardholders
- Board not reviewing financial information
- No evidence of audit or internal control monitoring
- Conflicts of interest not disclosed or addressed
- Expenditure approvals inconsistent with policy
- Missing board approval for required large purchases

## Governance Questions

1. **Board Meeting Frequency:** How often does the Board meet? Are financial reports presented at each meeting?

2. **Approval Authority:** Who can approve what? Are approval limits reasonable? Is there adequate segregation of duties?

3. **Conflicts of Interest:** Does the library have a conflicts policy? Are board members and staff required to disclose conflicts?

4. **Audit:** Does the library have annual audits? Are audit findings addressed? Are corrective actions implemented?

5. **Financial Reporting:** Is financial information presented to the Board regularly? Does the Board receive detailed or summary reports?

6. **Policies:** Are policies updated regularly? Are policies reviewed and approved by the Board?

7. **Controls:** Are internal controls documented? Is there evidence of monitoring or testing?

8. **Segregation of Duties:** Is there adequate separation between those requesting, approving, and paying for items?

## Cross-Reference Guide

Compare FOIA-05 findings to:

- FOIA-01: Are budgets presented to Board? Are budget-versus-actual reports prepared?
- FOIA-02: Are card expenditures documented per policy? Do card users follow approval procedures?
- FOIA-03: Are cash receipts handled per policy? Are deposits timely?
- FOIA-04: Are Foundation Fund expenditures authorized per policy? Are Board approvals documented?

## Follow-Up Items

After reviewing FOIA-05 and comparing to other documents:

- Request documentation for policy exceptions or deviations
- Ask for specific Board approval records if not provided
- Request explanation for absent or missing controls
- Ask about any changes to policies or procedures
- Request documentation of conflict of interest disclosures
- Ask about audit findings and responses
