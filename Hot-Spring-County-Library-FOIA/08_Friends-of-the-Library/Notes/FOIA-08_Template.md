# FOIA-08: Friends of the Library (Template)

## Purpose

Request financial records involving the Friends of the Library or similar organizations that are held or controlled by a public entity (library or county).

## When to Use

This request should be completed **only in later stages**, after initial FOIA responses are analyzed and if the analysis suggests:

- Significant financial activity between the library and Friends organization
- Transfers or reimbursements involving the Friends
- Donations or fundraising proceeds managed by the Friends
- Property or equipment held by the Friends but used by the library
- Concerns about separate accounting or control issues
- Questions about restrictions on or use of Friends-managed funds

## Critical Instruction

**This request seeks only records possessed, maintained, or controlled by a public entity** (the library itself or a county office). It does NOT request records held by the Friends of the Library as a private organization—only records held by the library about Friends activities.

---

## Template Request

**Subject:** Freedom of Information Request - Friends of the Library Records

**Request Body:**

Pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-101 et seq., I request the following public records held by the Hot Spring County Public Library relating to the Friends of the Library:

### FINANCIAL TRANSFERS AND REIMBURSEMENTS

1. All records held by the library showing funds transferred between the Friends of the Library and the library, including date, amount, and purpose

2. All records held by the library showing reimbursements from the library to the Friends of the Library, including date, amount, and purpose

3. All records held by the library showing reimbursements from the Friends of the Library to the library, including date, amount, and purpose

### PURCHASES AND EXPENDITURES

4. All records held by the library showing purchases made by the Friends of the Library on behalf of the library, including vendor name, date, amount, and description of items

5. All receipts and invoices for purchases made by the Friends held in the library's possession

6. All records of funds or property donated to the library by the Friends, including date, amount or description, and any restrictions on use

### DONATIONS AND FUNDRAISING

7. All records held by the library documenting donations made to or through the Friends of the Library for the benefit of the library

8. All records held by the library showing fundraising proceeds collected by the Friends

9. All records held by the library identifying the sources of funds held or managed by the Friends

### RESTRICTED FUNDS AND PROPERTY

10. All records held by the library documenting restrictions placed on funds donated by or through the Friends

11. All records showing donations made by the Friends to the library that were subject to restrictions on their use

12. All records documenting property transferred from the Friends to the library, including description, date, and value

13. Records showing current ownership and control of property obtained through the Friends

### FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND REPORTS

14. All financial reports, statements, or summaries held by the library relating to the Friends of the Library

15. All records held by the library showing the financial condition or activities of the Friends

### GOVERNANCE AND AUTHORIZATION

16. All records held by the library documenting authorization or approval for transfers or expenditures involving the Friends

17. All Board minutes or resolutions held by the library relating to the Friends of the Library

### SEPARATE ACCOUNTING

18. Records documenting whether the Friends of the Library maintains separate accounting from the library's accounts

19. Records showing bank accounts held in the name of the Friends of the Library and controlled by the library

20. Records documenting the library's involvement in or control of Friends finances

### PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

Please provide records in the following manner:
- Electronic records in their existing electronic format
- Chronological order with a cover sheet identifying each document
- For any withheld or redacted portions, identify the specific statutory exemption for each
- Written confirmation if no responsive records exist in the library's possession
- Contact information for any other public custodian holding responsive records

If reproduction costs exceed $25, please provide an itemized estimate before producing records. I will pay all reasonable reproduction costs.

## Key Distinctions

### This Request Seeks:
- Records held or controlled by the public library
- Records the library maintains about Friends activities
- Financial records involving both the library and the Friends
- Board-approved transfers or authorization
- Public entity accounts held for Friends purposes

### This Request Does NOT Seek:
- Private records held solely by the Friends organization
- Personnel records of Friends officers or employees
- Internal Friends communications not involving the library
- Records not in the library's custody or control
- Minutes of Friends-only meetings (unless held by the library)
- Private donations not involving the library

## Analysis Framework

When records arrive, analyze:

1. **Separation of Funds:** Are Friends funds held separately from library operating funds?

2. **Accounting:** How are Friends transactions accounted for? Are they included in library financial statements?

3. **Control:** Who controls Friends financial accounts? Does the library have signatory authority?

4. **Authorization:** Are library transfers or expenditures to/from Friends approved by the Board?

5. **Restrictions:** Are donations to the library through the Friends subject to restrictions?

6. **Transparency:** Are Friends activities disclosed in library financial reports?

7. **Property:** Does the library own property acquired through the Friends? How is ownership documented?

8. **Compliance:** Do Friends transactions comply with library policy?

## Red Flags

Watch for:

- Large transfers without documented Board approval
- Donations with restrictions not honored or documented
- Friends accounts commingled with library operating accounts
- Library employees receiving benefits from Friends
- Property donated by Friends not documented in library inventory
- Missing financial records for Friends-related activity
- Unexplained transfers between library and Friends accounts
- Inconsistent accounting of Friends transactions
- Changes in Friends structure without Board approval
