# Arkansas Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) - Quick Reference

## Legal Authority

**Arkansas Code Annotated § 25-19-101 et seq.** (Arkansas Freedom of Information Act)

The law establishes the right of the public to access public records maintained by public bodies, with limited statutory exemptions.

## Key Definitions

**Public Body:** Any state or local governmental agency, board, commission, authority, or other entity that is funded by tax dollars or created by law. This includes public libraries.

**Public Record:** Any document, paper, letter, book, map, tape, photograph, film, or other material (including electronic records) that is made or received by a public body and is required to be opened to public inspection under the state's FOIA law.

## Right of Access

- **Who:** Any person (no residency requirement, no specific reason required)
- **What:** Any public record
- **When:** During business hours
- **How:** Written or oral request
- **Cost:** Reasonable reproduction costs only

## Response Timelines

**Acknowledgment:** Public body should acknowledge receipt without unreasonable delay

**Response:** Normally within **3 business days** of receipt
- May be extended for good cause
- If extension needed, must notify requester

**Partial Responses:** May provide partial response if some records are exempt

## Standard Language to Include in Requests

### Request Format
Use Arkansas FOIA citation: **Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-101 et seq.**

Example:
> "Pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-101 et seq., I hereby request..."

### Production Requirements

Standard provisions to include:

1. **Electronic Format**
   > "For records maintained in electronic format, please provide in the existing electronic format, including native spreadsheets or database exports where readily available."

2. **Exemption Identification**
   > "For any portion of a record that is withheld or redacted, please identify the specific statutory exemption claimed for each withholding or redaction."

3. **No Records Confirmation**
   > "If no responsive records exist, please provide written confirmation that no such records are maintained or available."

4. **Alternative Custodian**
   > "If any responsive records are held by another public custodian, please identify that custodian and provide contact information."

5. **Fee Estimate**
   > "If reproduction costs exceed $25, please provide an itemized estimate of costs before producing the records. I will indicate whether I wish to proceed."

## Exemptions

The Arkansas FOIA provides limited exemptions. Records CAN be withheld only if:

### Statutory Exemptions
- Information specifically prohibited from disclosure by federal or state law
- Proprietary or trade secret information
- Information related to active law enforcement investigations
- Attorney-client privileged communications
- Information involving personal privacy (specific narrow definition)
- Information about juvenile delinquency proceedings
- Information about certain dependent children matters

### Important Note
- Exemptions are **narrowly construed**
- Burden is on the public body to justify the exemption
- If only part of a record is exempt, non-exempt portions must be released with exemption identified

## Request Procedures

### How to Submit

**Email:** (recommended and fastest)
- Address to public records custodian or clerk
- Subject line: "FOIA Request"
- Include all request details

**Mail:** Certified mail to library address

**In Person:** Library public services desk
- Make request during business hours
- Ask for receipt

### Response Options

**Public Body Must:**
1. Comply with the request fully, OR
2. Explain refusal and cite the exemption, OR
3. Request extension (with notice to requester)

**Cannot Say:**
- "No such records"... without verification
- "Too burdensome"... without calculation
- "Costs are too high"... without estimate

### Fees

**What Can Be Charged:**
- Reasonable reproduction costs only (copying, printing, postage, etc.)
- Labor costs only in certain limited circumstances and only if costs exceed $25

**What Cannot Be Charged:**
- Search fees
- Review fees
- Administration fees
- Fees to discourage requests

**Fee Estimate:**
- If estimated costs exceed $25, must provide written estimate
- Requester can then agree or modify request
- Cannot proceed without agreement

## Your Rights

### As a Requester, You Have:

1. **Right to Access:** Public records without stating a reason
2. **Right to Partial Release:** If part of a record is exempt, non-exempt portions must be released
3. **Right to Know Exemptions:** Public body must identify the specific exemption claimed
4. **Right to Reasonable Fees:** Only actual reproduction costs
5. **Right to Appeal:** If request is denied or exemptions are claimed improperly
6. **Right to Redaction Requirements:** Ask that exempt portions be redacted rather than withholding entire records

### As a Requester, You Should:

1. **Be Specific:** Clearly describe the records
2. **Set Reasonable Scope:** Balance completeness with specificity
3. **Use Standard Language:** Reference the statute
4. **Document Everything:** Keep copies of requests and responses
5. **Follow Up:** Track timelines and follow up if no response
6. **Request Extensions:** Ask for clarification if scope is unclear

## If Denied

### Steps to Take:

1. **Ask for Written Explanation:** Why records are withheld and which exemption applies
2. **Ask for Partial Release:** Request that non-exempt portions be released with exemptions identified
3. **Appeal to Custodian's Superior:** Request reconsideration by supervisor
4. **File Complaint:** With Arkansas Attorney General
5. **Seek Legal Remedy:** Court action to compel release (rare, but available)

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Issue: "That will take too long"
**Solution:** Offer to narrow the request; ask for extension with timeline

### Issue: "Costs are too high"
**Solution:** Ask for itemized estimate; narrow the request; offer to pay; ask for best-available format

### Issue: "It's not a public record"
**Solution:** Ask for explanation in writing; cite statute; request clarification

### Issue: "We lost those records"
**Solution:** Ask for certification in writing; this itself becomes a public record

### Issue: "Attorney-client privilege"
**Solution:** Ask what portion is privileged; request release of non-privileged portions; can assert privilege narrowly

## Tips for Success

1. **Professional Tone:** Keep requests neutral and professional
2. **Specific Dates:** Use exact date ranges
3. **Clear Description:** Describe records by type, not subject matter
4. **Reasonable Scope:** Don't ask for things that don't exist or are overly burdensome
5. **Follow Up:** Track responses and follow up if timeline is exceeded
6. **Document:** Keep all communications and track timelines
7. **Be Patient:** Good-faith responses take time
