# FOIA-07: Targeted Communications (Template)

## Purpose

Request communications that may explain, document, or clarify specific financial issues or transactions identified during the analysis of earlier FOIA responses.

## When to Use

This request should be completed **only after** analysis of FOIA-01 through FOIA-05 responses identifies specific financial discrepancies or issues requiring explanation, such as:

- Missing documentation for specific transactions
- Unusual expenditures
- Unexplained transfers or deposits
- Policy deviations
- Audit findings not addressed
- Questionable vendor relationships
- Cash or reconciliation issues

## Request Structure

The request should use precise names, dates, vendors, amounts, or subjects whenever possible, not broad searches.

---

**Subject:** Freedom of Information Request - Targeted Communications

**Request Body:**

Pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-101 et seq., I request the following public records:

**[ISSUE 1]: [Description of Issue]**

1. All email messages, text messages, and written communications between [person/department] and [person/department] from [date range] relating to [specific transaction, vendor, or issue], including:
   - All attachments
   - Calendar invitations
   - Responses and replies

**[ISSUE 2]: [Description of Issue]**

2. All communications from [person/department] to [person/department] dated [dates] regarding [specific subject], including:
   - [Specific document types]

**[ISSUE 3]: [Description of Issue]**

3. All records (including emails, memos, or letters) concerning [specific topic], such as:
   - Communications about [vendor name]
   - Communications regarding [account name or fund]
   - Communications about [policy or procedure issue]

## Template Sections

### Communications About Missing Documentation

If FOIA-02 or prior analysis revealed missing receipts or documentation:

> All email messages and written communications from [librarian/staff member] to [board member/county official] from [date range] concerning the charge/transaction of $[amount] on [date] for [description], including any discussions about missing receipts, documentation, or approval of the transaction.

### Communications About Specific Expenditures

If an unusual expenditure was identified:

> All email messages and written communications from [date range] among library staff and board members concerning the purchase or expenditure of [vendor/item/amount], including any discussions about the purpose, authorization, or propriety of the expenditure.

### Communications About Audit Issues

If audit findings were not addressed:

> All email messages and written communications from [date range] between library staff and the library's auditor regarding the audit findings in the [year] audit, including communications about corrective actions or responses to identified deficiencies.

### Communications About Policy Changes or Exceptions

If policy deviations were noted:

> All email messages and written communications from [date range] between the library director and board members concerning the [policy name] policy, including discussions about changes to the policy or exceptions to the policy.

### Communications About Vendor Issues

If unusual vendor relationships or transactions were identified:

> All email messages and written communications from [date range] between library staff and [vendor name], including order confirmations, invoices, and any discussions about products, services, pricing, or payment.

### Communications About Fund Transfers

If unexplained transfers were identified:

> All email messages and written communications from [date range] among library staff and board members concerning the transfer of funds from [fund name] to [fund name], including any approval, authorization, or explanation for the transfer.

### Communications About Missing Records

If the FOIA responses revealed missing documents or procedures:

> All email messages and written communications from [date range] among library staff concerning [missing type of record], including discussions about where such records are maintained, how long they are retained, or why they are not available.

## Key Principles

### Be Specific
- Name the individuals involved
- Specify the date range (narrow if possible)
- Reference specific transactions, vendors, or issues
- Do not conduct "fishing expeditions"

### Request Only Public Records
- Communications related to library business
- Do not request private personnel information
- Do not request attorney-client privileged communications
- Do not request advice sought for legal proceedings

### Include All Formats
- Email messages
- Text messages
- Memos
- Letters
- Calendar invitations
- Attachments

### Use "And"
Request communications between Person A AND Person B, not communications by either person (which would be overboard).

## FOIA-07 Should NOT Include

- Broad searches ("all communications about finances")
- Requests for private, personnel-related communications
- Requests for attorney-client privileged advice
- Requests to create new records or compilations
- Accusations or inflammatory language
- Communications that might violate privacy
- Communications about the FOIA request itself

## Examples of Appropriate Requests

**Good:**
> All email messages between the Library Director and the Board President from January 1 to March 31, 2024, concerning the purchase of office equipment from [vendor name] on [date].

**Too Broad:**
> All emails about spending or financial matters.

**Good:**
> All written communications from the Library Director to the Finance Committee dated between [date] and [date] regarding the audit findings in the 2023 audit.

**Too Broad:**
> All communications about audits.
