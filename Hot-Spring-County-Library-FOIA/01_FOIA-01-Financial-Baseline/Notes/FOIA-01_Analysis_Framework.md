# FOIA-01: Financial Baseline Analysis Framework

## Purpose

Establish the financial baseline and identify all funds, accounts, recurring vendors, revenue sources, and major expenditure categories.

## Records Expected

### Budgets
- **What to examine:** Compare year-to-year budgets to identify new accounts, removed accounts, major funding changes, and approved spending levels by category
- **Key questions:** Are budgets accurate? Do amendments match actual spending? Are all changes documented?

### General Ledgers and Expenditure/Revenue Ledgers
- **What to examine:** Account structure, fund names, expenditure categories, and revenue sources; cross-check account balances against financial statements
- **Key questions:** How many funds? What are they for? Are balances reconciled monthly? Are entries timely?

### Check Registers and Warrant Registers
- **What to examine:** Chronological list of all payments; identify payees, amounts, dates, and approval; match to supporting invoices and receipts
- **Key questions:** Are all checks recorded? Are amounts accurate? Are payees legitimate? Are there unusual payment patterns?

### Monthly, Quarterly, and Annual Financial Statements
- **What to examine:** Balance sheets, income statements, and fund statements; reconcile against general ledgers and check registers
- **Key questions:** Do statements tie to ledgers? Are all accounts included? Are fund balances accurate? Are statements timely?

### Budget-Versus-Actual Reports
- **What to examine:** Variance analysis showing where actual spending exceeded or fell short of budget; identify categories with major variances
- **Key questions:** Are variances explained? Are there patterns of over-spending? Are amendments used appropriately?

### Board Financial Reports
- **What to examine:** What financial information is presented to the Board; how detailed; how frequent
- **Key questions:** Does the Board receive complete information? Are financial reports presented on a regular schedule? Are concerns raised?

### Bank Accounts and Financial Accounts
- **What to examine:** List of all accounts, account numbers, authorized signatories, purpose, and current balances
- **Key questions:** How many accounts? Who has access? Are accounts documented? Do accounts match the fund structure?

## What to Track

As documents arrive, organize them by:

1. **Fund/Account**
   - Identify all funds and accounts
   - Note purpose and authorized spending categories
   - Track beginning balance, activity, and ending balance

2. **Expenditure Categories**
   - Identify all categories of spending
   - Group by fund and category
   - Calculate total spending by category

3. **Recurring Vendors**
   - Identify vendors receiving regular payments
   - Track frequency and amounts
   - Note vendor categories (utilities, supplies, services, etc.)

4. **Account Reconciliation**
   - Document reconciliation process and frequency
   - Compare ledger balances to bank statements
   - Identify reconciling items and timing issues

5. **Revenue Sources**
   - Identify all sources and amounts
   - Track by fund
   - Verify deposits match ledger entries

## Red Flags in Financial Baseline

Watch for (document but do not assume misconduct):

- Missing months or gaps in financial statements
- Unexplained account balances or changes
- Accounts not matching the approved fund structure
- Ledger entries without corresponding bank payments
- Missing reconciliation documents or procedures
- Unusual account activity or transfers
- Expenditure categories not matching approved budget
- Revenue sources not documented
- Missing supporting documentation for transactions
- Delays in financial reporting or reconciliation

## Analysis Checklist

- [ ] All budgets received and reviewed
- [ ] General ledgers obtained for all funds
- [ ] Ledger balances reconciled to financial statements
- [ ] All accounts identified and documented
- [ ] Authorized users and signatories identified
- [ ] Expenditure categories mapped
- [ ] Recurring vendors identified
- [ ] Monthly reconciliation procedures understood
- [ ] Bank reconciliation reviewed
- [ ] All revenue sources documented
- [ ] Timeline of financial reporting established
- [ ] Unusual items flagged for follow-up

## Follow-Up Questions for Next FOIA Request

Based on baseline documents, FOIA-02 will request:
- Credit card and debit card statements to detail expenditures
- Supporting receipts and invoices to verify transactions
- Reimbursement records to verify expense claims
