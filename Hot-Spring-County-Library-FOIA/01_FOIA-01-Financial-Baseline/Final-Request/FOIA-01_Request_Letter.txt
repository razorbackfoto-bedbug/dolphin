SUBJECT: Freedom of Information Request—Financial Records (January 1, 2023 through Present)

Dear Public Records Custodian:

Pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-101 et seq., I hereby request the following public records in the custody of the Hot Spring County Public Library:

1. Annual budgets and all amended budgets for the fiscal years 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 (through present);

2. General ledgers for all accounts and funds maintained by or for the library, for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

3. Expenditure ledgers and revenue ledgers for all accounts and funds, for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

4. Check registers showing all checks issued and cleared, for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

5. Warrant registers or payment authorization records, for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

6. Monthly financial statements for all months January 2023 through present;

7. Quarterly financial statements for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

8. Annual financial statements or year-end financial reports for fiscal years 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 (through present);

9. Budget-versus-actual reports or variance reports for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

10. Any financial reports presented to the Library Board of Directors, including board meeting materials containing financial information, for the period January 1, 2023 through present;

11. Records identifying all bank accounts, investment accounts, depository accounts, and other financial accounts maintained by or in the name of the library, or held in trust for the library, current as of the date of this request;

12. For electronically maintained records (items 2–10 above), please provide these records in their existing electronic format, including native spreadsheets or accounting system exports where such formats are readily available.

Request for Production:

I request that these records be produced as follows:

- Electronic records should be provided in electronic format where readily available;
- Original documents and electronically generated records should be provided in native format (native Excel spreadsheets, native accounting system exports, etc.) rather than converted to PDF, where practically available;
- Records should be provided in chronological order, with a cover sheet or index identifying each document;
- If any portion of a record is withheld or redacted, please identify the specific statutory exemption claimed for each withholding or redaction;
- If no records responsive to any portion of this request exist, please provide a written statement confirming that no such records exist;
- If any responsive records are held by another public custodian, please identify that custodian and provide contact information.

Reproduction Costs:

If the cost of reproduction exceeds $25, please provide an itemized estimate of costs before producing the records, and I will indicate whether I wish to proceed.

I will pay all applicable reasonable reproduction costs.

This request seeks records in the possession, custody, or control of the Hot Spring County Public Library and any records maintained by the library or on the library's behalf.

I expect a response to this request within the time required by the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act.
