# FOIA-04: Library Foundation Fund Analysis Framework

## Purpose

Independently reconcile the fund's beginning balances, contributions, investment earnings, expenditures, transfers, and ending balances. Verify that Foundation Fund assets are held separately, used appropriately, and accounted for correctly.

## Reconciliation Strategy

For each year and for the full period:

**Beginning Balance + Deposits + Investment Earnings - Expenditures - Transfers = Ending Balance**

This fundamental formula must balance. Any discrepancy requires explanation.

## Key Documents to Organize

### Fund Identification
- Account name and number
- Type of account (savings, checking, investment)
- Account custodian/bank
- Authorized signatories
- Restrictions or donor intent
- Stated purpose of the fund

### Beginning Balances
- Balance as of January 1, 2020
- Any prior-period activity affecting opening balance

### Deposits and Contributions
- Date received
- Source (donor name, grant, transfer from operating fund, investment earnings, etc.)
- Amount
- Any restrictions or donor intent

### Investment Activity
- Beginning investment balance
- Purchases of securities
- Sales of securities
- Investment earnings (interest, dividends, gains/losses)
- Ending investment balance

### Withdrawals and Expenditures
- Date
- Payee
- Amount
- Purpose
- Authorization/approval

### Transfers
- Date
- Direction (to or from Foundation Fund)
- Amount
- Purpose
- Authorization/approval

### Ending Balances
- Cash balance at end of period
- Investment balance at end of period
- Total fund balance

## What to Track

Create a reconciliation schedule for each fiscal year:

| Description | Amount |
|---|---|
| Beginning Balance (Jan 1) | |
| + Deposits/Contributions | |
| + Investment Earnings | |
| - Expenditures | |
| - Transfers (out) | |
| = Ending Balance | |

Compare to:
- Bank statements (cash portion)
- Investment statements (investment portion)
- Financial statements

## Red Flags in Foundation Fund Records

Watch for (document but do not assume misconduct):

- Deposits without donor documentation or intent
- Expenditures without formal approval or authorization
- Transfers to or from the fund without explanation
- Fund balances not reconciling
- Missing bank or investment statements
- Expenditures inconsistent with stated fund purpose
- Donations with restrictions not honored
- Investment earnings not documented
- Missing financial statements for certain years
- Large withdrawals without supporting documentation
- Fund account commingled with operating accounts
- Transfers that may violate donor intent
- Missing board approval for major expenditures or transfers
- Expenditures from Foundation Fund for operating expenses
- No evidence of separate accounting
- Donations deposited but not recorded
- Investment accounts without statements
- Signatory changes without documentation

## Analysis Questions

For the Foundation Fund as a whole:

1. **Separation:** Is the Foundation Fund held in a separate account?
2. **Accounting:** Is the Foundation Fund accounted for separately from the operating fund?
3. **Restrictions:** Are donor restrictions documented and honored?
4. **Balance:** Does the fund balance reconcile year to year?
5. **Deposits:** Are all donations deposited and recorded?
6. **Earnings:** Are investment earnings documented and recorded?
7. **Expenditures:** Are all expenditures authorized and appropriate?
8. **Transfers:** Are transfers approved and documented?
9. **Purpose:** Are expenditures consistent with the stated purpose?
10. **Governance:** Are there Board approvals for major transactions?

For each deposit or expenditure:

1. **Documentation:** Is there supporting documentation?
2. **Amount:** Is the amount accurate?
3. **Purpose:** Is the stated purpose appropriate?
4. **Authorization:** Is there evidence of approval?
5. **Timing:** Is the transaction timely and current?
6. **Consistency:** Is this consistent with past practice?

## Analysis Checklist

- [ ] Foundation Fund account identified
- [ ] Account number and custodian documented
- [ ] Authorized signatories identified
- [ ] Restrictions and purpose documented
- [ ] Bank statements obtained for all years
- [ ] All deposits documented and reconciled
- [ ] All expenditures documented
- [ ] All transfers documented and approved
- [ ] Investment statements obtained (if applicable)
- [ ] Investment earnings calculated and verified
- [ ] Reconciliation schedule completed for each year
- [ ] Total fund balances verified
- [ ] Donor intent letters reviewed (if applicable)
- [ ] Board approvals reviewed
- [ ] Expenditures reviewed for consistency with purpose
- [ ] Unusual transactions flagged

## Follow-Up Items

After analyzing FOIA-04 responses:

- Investigate any variance in fund balances
- Request explanation for large or unusual expenditures
- Verify donor intent for restricted gifts
- Review authorization for any major transfers
- Investigate missing bank or investment statements
- Verify investment earnings calculations
- Request board minutes if major expenditures lack documentation
